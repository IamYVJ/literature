How to play

  1. Two teams sit alternately, so the players either side of you are opponents.
  2. A set is half a suit: 2–7 low, 9–A high. Eight sets of six cards, or nine if the host keeps the 8s in.
  3. On your turn, ask one opponent for one card. You must already hold a card of that set, and you cannot ask for a card you hold.
  4. If they have it they hand it over and you ask again. If they do not, the turn passes to them.
  5. Claim a set by naming who on your team holds each card. Right and it is yours; wrong and it goes to the other team.
  6. The first team to take the majority of the sets wins.
